# Product overview

## One-line pitch

VAELKOR CIVIC is a civic infrastructure network where people report real-world problems, communities verify them, legitimate executors receive structured work orders, and completed work is proven before a case is closed.

## Core product sentence

> VAELKOR CIVIC transforms citizen observations into verified infrastructure action.

## The problem

Cities fail not because nobody notices broken roads, garbage piles, blocked drains, or dark streets — but because **observation and resolution are disconnected**. Complaints vanish into queues. CIVIC keeps a continuous chain from real-world problem to verified resolution.

## Core loop

```
Observe → Verify → Scope → Assign → Execute → Prove → Verify → Close
```

Citizens act as distributed sensors. The platform structures evidence into cases, issues work orders to authorized executors, and requires before/during/after proof plus inspection before closure.

## MVP categories

1. Roads  
2. Garbage  
3. Drainage  
4. Streetlights  

## What MVP proves

> Can CIVIC turn a citizen-observed physical problem into a structured, verifiable work order and demonstrate its complete lifecycle?

## Roles (MVP)

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| Citizen | Report, confirm, add evidence, follow cases |
| Contractor | Browse available work, accept, submit execution evidence |
| Inspector | Review before/after, pass or fail |

Government, funding, NGOs, and AI classification are out of scope for this MVP. Government remains an optional integration later — not a dependency.

## Principles

1. Evidence over claims  
2. Action over reporting  
3. Verification over assumption  
4. Coordination over centralization  
5. Transparency over ambiguity
